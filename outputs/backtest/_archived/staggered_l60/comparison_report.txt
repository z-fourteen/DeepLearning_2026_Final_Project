--- WHY STAGGERED UNDERPERFORMS ---

1. CONCENTRATION RISK: Each bucket holds only K/N = 4 stocks
   -> Extreme idiosyncratic volatility per bucket
   -> MaxDD -83% vs baseline -18%

2. VOLATILITY COMPOUNDING: Daily compounding amplifies variance
   -> IR drops from 0.200 to 0.072 despite higher absolute returns

3. SIGNAL DILUTION: Portfolio holds mixed-age signals (Day 0..4)
   -> Model predicts 5-day returns but buckets enter on different dates

4. EXTREME BULL MARKET: Benchmark +969% ann (daily view) during test
   -> Lower-beta strategy underperforms massively in relative terms

--- WHERE STAGGERED COULD HELP ---
   - Smoother P&L distribution (no single bad rebalance day)
   - Better execution liquidity (smaller orders each day)
   - Faster regime-change reaction

--- IMPROVEMENT OPTIONS ---
   A. Increase K significantly (K>=50 so each bucket >=10 stocks)
   B. Reduce num_buckets to 3 (larger buckets)
   C. Overlapping windows instead of hard partitioning
   D. Retrain with 1-day label for pure daily strategy
